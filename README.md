## 👋 About me

I am an AI researcher with a focus on healthcare. I explore where AI can add the most value in medicine, so that every patient benefits from accurate, timely and trustworthy diagnosis. I care about taking ideas from research into real-world use, with explainability and clinician oversight built in.

## 🚀 Selected work

**🦴 LumbarAI** &nbsp;·&nbsp; automated grading of 11 lumbar MRI features
Swin3D transformer, PACS and DICOM integration, explainability heatmaps, clinician correction with audit trail, and bilingual (English and Italian) draft reports. In routine use at Poliambulatorio Oberdan, Brescia.

**🧭 Multimodal risk stratification in spine health**
Radiological severity combined with patient-reported outcomes and unsupervised clustering, to identify patient risk profiles and support personalised care.

**👶 Infant brain MRI segmentation**
Leveraging adult brain priors to address data scarcity in neonatal neuroimaging. [arXiv:2510.09306](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.09306)
